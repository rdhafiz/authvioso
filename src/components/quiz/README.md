# quiz

Intro, question, navigation, results, answer explanations.

The constraints here are unusual, so read them before building:

- **No timer.** Anywhere. Timing measures how recently you read something, not
  whether you understand it.
- Answers stay changeable until submit, and submit is always deliberate —
  nothing auto-advances or auto-submits.
- Explanations appear **after** the whole thing is submitted. Per-question
  feedback steers people through the rest of the attempt.
- Results are "here's what you've got and haven't got", not a score. No
  percentage in a big font.
- Wrong answers link to the specific concept, not the whole chapter.
- Mark correct/incorrect with an icon and a word, not just green and red.
- No streaks, no progress bar implying pace, no congratulations.
