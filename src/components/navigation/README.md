# navigation

Header, footer, sidebar, breadcrumbs, chapter next/previous, search trigger,
language and theme switches.

Prerequisites and "related concepts" are **generated from the knowledge
graph**, not listed here. That's the important part: a hand-maintained
prerequisite list drifts from the chapters and you find out when a reader
follows a link to something that no longer explains what they needed.

Other things worth keeping in mind:

- The header holds four items. It's been four since the start and it should
  stay four.
- Show where you are without making people click to find out.
- Every page needs somewhere to go next. Dead ends are a bug.
- Cap suggestion lists at five and say why each one is there — an unexplained
  list of links is work for the reader.
- Never rank suggestions by what other people clicked.
